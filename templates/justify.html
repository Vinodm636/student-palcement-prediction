Why F1 is the Key Metric
Our dataset has imbalanced classes (~44% placed, ~56% not placed). In such scenarios:

• Accuracy can be misleading — a model that always predicts "Not Placed" scores ~56% accuracy without learning anything.

• F1-Score = harmonic mean of Precision & Recall. It penalises models that sacrifice one for the other.

• ROC-AUC measures rank discrimination across all thresholds — ideal for probabilistic placement prediction.
